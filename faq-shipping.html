FAQ & Shipping

Helpful answers for ordering, payment, delivery, and caring for your Terra Haven purchase.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the Terra Haven collection, select your preferred product and options, add it to your cart, enter your shipping details, and complete payment securely through Razorpay.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payments are securely processed through Razorpay. Available payment options are displayed during checkout.

How will I know my order was placed successfully?

After successful payment verification, an order confirmation will be shown on the website and the order details will be sent to Terra Haven for processing.

How long will delivery take?

Delivery times depend on the destination, product availability, and courier service. Tracking information will be shared once the shipment is processed.

Do you ship across India?

Yes. Orders can be delivered to serviceable locations across India.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Contact Terra Haven as soon as possible. Changes can only be made before the order enters the shipping process.

How can I track my order?

Shipping is currently processed through our logistics partner. Tracking details will be shared once available.

Shipping

Orders are packed carefully and dispatched after payment confirmation. Delivery timelines may vary depending on destination, courier conditions, and product availability.

Need more help?

For order questions, keep your order ID ready when you contact Terra Haven.
